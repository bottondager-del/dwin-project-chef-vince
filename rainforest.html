Rainforest Kichene

Known for its luscious green scenery, Rainforest Kichene provides a cornucopia of international and local flavours perfectly combined into what is known as the Food Haven of Pampanga. From the signature Salted Crust Lechon Belly, USDA Certified Angus Steaks, signature Paellas, and its all-time classic organic salad a guaranteed farm to table restaurant dealing with the freshest of ingredients perfectly and personally prepared by in house chef, Chef Vince Garcia.

MONSTERA

Monstera can accommodate up to 15 persons, Maximum. It is also the main dining area of the restaurant

TILLANDSIA

Tillandsia can accommodate up to 10 persons, maximum. Good for Small gatherings, small group meetings.

ANTHURIUM
