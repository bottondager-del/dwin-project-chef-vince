CHEF VINCE GARCIA CULINARY GROUP PREPARED 250K
WORTH OF FOOD PACKS FOR FRONTLINERS IN PAMPANGA

May 13, 2020 by admin



Nearly two months after launching the ‘Cook for Heroes’ campaign, this Kapampangan chef, the president of Chef Vince Garcia Culinary Group has been into roving into hospitals in Pampanga to deliver home-cooked meals to frontliners.




As of May 05, Chef Vince Garcia was able to personally hand-over P250,000 worth of food packs to medical institutions, police authorities, city marshals, barangay officials and other volunteers in the City of San Fernando and Angeles City.

This young Fernandino restaurateur has been closely working with community programs of volunteer organizations like Lugud Balen, Pamisaupan Kapampangan, Tulong Abe and the Rotary Club of San Fernando, And chef Sau del rosario celebrity chef in reaching out to the needy.


“More than the thought that we appreciate their hardwork and sacrifice, sharing them our homecooked specials is our way of boosting their mood and determination. Being in the frontline is a tough task, they work 24 hours away from their families to help the government in flattening the curve of this pandemic affecting us. I hope that this humble initiative of Chef Vince Garcia Culinary Group will remind them how we are here for them,” he explained.

On behalf of CVGCG, our very own chef would like to extend his utmost gratitude to all the individuals who have shared their resources in making this project sustaine

“NutriAsia has been also my partner in giving out to the communities ( and private donors who remain private )Support is showering from varied walks of life. With this, I will devotedly cook for our heroes until the lockdown will be lifted. Seeing the priceless smiles of our frontliners is way rewarding,” he added.
